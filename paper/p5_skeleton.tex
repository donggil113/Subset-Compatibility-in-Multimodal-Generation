\section{Introduction}
Any-to-any generators expose many conditionals of one implied joint. Under the
same observation $x$, a target $z$ can be produced directly, $z\sim q_d(z\mid x)$,
or through an intermediate, $y\sim q(y\mid x)$, $z\sim q(z\mid x,y)$.
The marginalisation identity is standard; the question is whether a learned
model satisfies it once numerical, guidance and Monte Carlo error are
separated out. \todo{P5-MODEL-RESULTS}

Contributions (provisional; each must survive the model phase):
(i) a measurement protocol with an explicit Monte Carlo noise floor and the
conditioning example as the independent unit;
(ii) controls showing what the protocol cannot see (joint breaks with exact
target marginals, collapse, observed intermediates);
(iii) \todo{P5-MODEL-RESULTS} measurements on learned generators;
(iv) \todo{P5-METHOD} whether the gap can be reduced without quality loss
against shared-joint and equal-extra-compute baselines.

\section{Problem setup}
Sequential law $q_s(z\mid x)=\int q(z\mid x,y)\,q(y\mid x)\,dy$;
target gap $\Delta_T(x)=D(q_d(\cdot\mid x),q_s(\cdot\mid x))$;
joint gap $\Delta_J(x)$ against a direct joint sampler.

\begin{proposition}[elementary]
The triple $(q(y\mid x), q(z\mid x,y), q_d(z\mid x))$ is compatible iff
$q_s(\cdot\mid x)=q_d(\cdot\mid x)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proposition}[elementary]
$\Delta_T\equiv 0$ does not imply $\Delta_J\equiv 0$ (Gaussian construction).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proposition}[elementary]
Collapsed generators have $\Delta_T=\Delta_J=0$; a strictly proper score
separates them from the truth. Using the observed intermediate improves the
proper score by information leakage.
\end{proposition}

\section{Measurement protocol}
Per conditioning example: two independent reference sample sets $A,B$ and the
compared set $C$; statistic $d_i=\mathrm{ED}(A_i,C_i)-\mathrm{ED}(A_i,B_i)$,
symmetric under $H_0$; one-sided sign-flip test over examples. Proper score
(CRPS / energy score), fidelity and diversity reported alongside. Same-index
paired MSE is not used.
